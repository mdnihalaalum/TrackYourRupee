Welcome to TrackYourRupee

Offline-First Personal Daily Ledger

  • 1. Welcome & About TrackYourRupee:
    TrackYourRupee is a simple, fast personal expense tracker that helps you manage your monthly allowance, set spending caps per category, track daily spending across Cash, UPI Lite, and UPI Bank, and automatically carry forward unspent balances every month.
  • 2. How Your Data Is Saved & Backed Up:
    Everything you add is saved right on your device so the app works offline anytime. Use Backup & Restore to save a full .json backup file (including budgets, caps, shortcuts, and bills) or export a .csv file for spreadsheets. If you connect Google Drive Backup, it only syncs one private expense.json file in your Drive.
  • 3. 100% Privacy & Safety:
    No external server or tracker ever collects or reads your financial details—everything stays strictly on your device and your own Google account. Tip: Avoid clearing browser site data unless you have exported a backup file first.
  • 4. Coming in Future Updates:
    * Support for bank statement linking may be added in future versions to make expense tracking even faster.
    * Smart budget planning and personalized guides to help you spend less will also be added.
